## Map tiles

The dashboards request map tiles from:

https://{s}.tile.openstreetmap.org/{z}/{x}/{y}.png

The dashboards display OpenStreetMap attribution. Applications using these tile servers must also follow the OpenStreetMap tile usage policy:

https://operations.osmfoundation.org/policies/tiles/

## Ambulance and healthcare-facility data

The local operational ambulance CSV and healthcare-facility CSV are not included in this public repository.

The healthcare-facility compilation used during development was assembled from third-party Wowsome pages and subsequently processed. It is not represented here as an official Government of Tamil Nadu publication.

The ambulance records were excluded pending privacy and redistribution-permission review.
